Which leathers to never turn into patchleather

No leather is cheaper or worse than patchleather — it is the cheapest, lowest-stat entry in every column. The recipe is 50 → 50 piece for piece; the money lost is the price gap, so the pricier the leather the heavier the loss

Quick rules

Both thrumbo leathers: never touch · value 3+: keep for clothes or sell · under 2 (birdskin, lightleather, pigskin, dog leather) is what belongs in a patch

Recipe: hand/electric tailor bench takes every leather except human leather (dread leather is fine) · if selling anyway, selling the original leather always pays more even for the cheapest — patch is for wearing yourself when scraps won't make a shirt, or for deliberately tanking value

Armor decided — which set fits the job

Power armor covers everything but the head and is made at the fabrication bench — pick by role: melee tanks take cataphract, shoot-and-move takes recon, space takes vacsuit, tight budgets layer flak; click headers to re-sort

Best mixes

Mid-game budget: flak vest + devilstrand duster + flak pants + flak helmet — 100% sharp on the torso before any power armor, because the vest layers under the duster · brutal endgame: legendary thrumbofur/hyperweave duster + flak vest beats cataphract on the torso (cataphract still wins on arms/legs) — a plain devilstrand shirt already gives 28% sharp despite being cloth

Orbital work (OD): see the spacewalk table below · extreme cold: parka + tuque, no armor needed

Spacewalks (OD) — which sets replace a vacsuit

Outside the ship is 100% vacuum at −75°C — the sets in the table come with enough cold insulation · every power armor gives the same +30%, so the helmet is the decider · the vacuum rating doesn't depend on quality · a sealed gravship is livable without a suit, but meteorites/drop-pod raids punch through the roof — keep spares · Biotech: the vacskin gland gene or Breathless resist vacuum with nothing worn

Hidden helmet bonus: the vacsuit helmet also blocks toxic — 80% toxic environment resistance + full tox gas immunity like a gas mask, plus better sharp armor; one item covers space and poison · 100% toxic never comes from a single piece — top up with a detoxifier lung (Biotech) or the tox-resistance gene, another stacking stat · the face mask also blocks toxic but only 50% and no tox gas
